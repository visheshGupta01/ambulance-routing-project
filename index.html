Algorithm Analysis

Simple explanation of the algorithms used.

Dijkstra's Algorithm

Models locations as vertices and roads as weighted edges. Finds the shortest distance from one location to all others.

Time: O(V²) in this implementation

Greedy Selection

Chooses the available ambulance with the smallest distance to the patient, then the nearest valid hospital.

Selection: minimum-cost valid candidate

Insertion Sort

Sorts candidate ambulance or hospital indexes by their route distances.

Worst case: O(n²)

Constraints

Busy ambulances, maintenance vehicles, full hospitals and unreachable locations are excluded.

Prevents invalid assignments
